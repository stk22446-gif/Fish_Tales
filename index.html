Our Philosophy

Where Nature Meets Wonder.

Fish Tales was founded with a singular dedication: to provide enthusiasts and keepers across India with ethically sourced, healthy exotic pets, vibrant show-quality aquarium fish, and complete living bioactive terrarium setups.

Whether you are an experienced hobbyist curating rare Cubaris isopod varieties, an aquarist appreciating the fine genetics of Dumbo Ear guppies, or introducing a bioactive naturalistic vivarium to your living space, Fish Tales provides healthy specimens with clear guidance.

Ethical Husbandry & Legal Compliance

Fish Tales strictly complies with all Indian environmental and wildlife protection legislation, including the Wildlife Protection Act (1972/2022). We do not trade in banned native wildlife. All livestock consist solely of captive-bred exotic invertebrates and certified commercial ornamental aquarium fish.
