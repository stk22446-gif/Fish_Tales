Boutique Breeders & Curators

Exotic Livestock & Bioactive Habitats.

Fish Tales is an independent boutique exotic livestock purveyor. We specialize in maintaining pure captive-bred bloodlines of fancy guppies, acclimatized Betta splendens, rare custodial isopods, and docile New World tarantulas.

Our bioactive enclosures and living kits are designed to replicate self-sustaining nature micro-habitats. We emphasize health, vigor, and accurate species identification over mass commercial volume.
