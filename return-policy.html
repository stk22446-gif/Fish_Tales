Return & Live Arrival Policy

Clear guidelines on live arrival assurance, unboxing verification, and replacement procedures for our living specimens.

📦 IMPORTANT: UNBOXING VIDEO & LIVE ARRIVAL POLICY

🎥 Unboxing Video Required
🚚 Replacement Shipping Charges Applicable

To protect both our customers and Fish Tales, please record a clear, continuous unboxing video while opening your delivered package.

  • ✓ Start recording before opening the sealed package.
  • ✓ Clearly show the outer packaging, shipping label and the complete unboxing process.
  • ✓ Do not pause, edit or cut the video.
  • ✓ If any live livestock arrives dead or damaged during transit, the unboxing video will be required as proof for verification.
  • ✓ Our team will review the video and verify the issue before approving a replacement.
Replacement Policy:

If a dead-on-arrival claim is verified and approved by Fish Tales, the replacement product will be provided free of product cost. However, the customer will need to pay the applicable delivery/shipping charges for the replacement shipment.

Please keep the unboxing video safe until your order is successfully verified.

Need to Submit an Unboxing Video or Have Questions?

Our team is available on WhatsApp to assist with transit tracking, unboxing video reviews, and livestock acclimatization guidance.
